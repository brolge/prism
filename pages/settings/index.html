Prism Lookup & Search

Prism Lookup is the built-in search engine. You can still pick Google, DuckDuckGo, or others below.

Passwords & Autofill

Save logins securely, autofill forms, and manage credentials. Use the built-in vault or connect Bitwarden / 1Password.

AutofillFills usernames and passwords on sites you trust.
Save promptsOffer to save new passwords after you sign in.
VaultView, reveal, import, or export saved logins.

Tip: choose Built-in password manager, then open “View saved passwords” to manage your vault.
